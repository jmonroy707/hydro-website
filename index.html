50 Years of History

For five decades, restaurateurs Alex and Gayle Dierkhising have helped shape Calistoga's culinary scene. Their journey began in the 1970s with the beloved Silverado Tavern, and continued across the street with the iconic, Wine Spectator "Grand Award"-winning All Seasons Bistro.

Building on that 50-year legacy of Napa Valley hospitality, they opened Hydro Grill in 1996. Today, it remains a favorite neighborhood watering hole, serving up hearty American classics, creative craft cocktails, and an extensive selection of local beers and wines.
