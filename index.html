Calistoga Since 1996

Our Story

For decades, restaurateurs Alex and Gayle Dierkhising have helped shape Calistoga's hospitality scene, earning recognition including a Wine Spectator Grand Award.

Opened in 1996, Hydro Grill remains a neighborhood watering hole in a historic brick building, serving American classics, craft cocktails and a wide selection of local beers.

A longtime Napa Valley neighborhood spot for food, drinks and local entertainment.
