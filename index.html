Our Story

For 50 years, restaurateurs Alex and Gayle Dierkhising have shaped Calistoga's culinary scene, earning the prestigious Wine Spectator "Grand Award" along the way.

Opened in 1996, Hydro Grill remains a favorite neighborhood watering hole. Join us in our historic brick building for hearty American classics, craft cocktails, and an extensive selection of local beers on tap.

50 continuous years in the Napa Valley hospitality business. Stop in and join the family.
